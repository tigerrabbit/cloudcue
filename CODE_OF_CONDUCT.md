# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and learners with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, discuss ideas in good faith, and give constructive criticism. Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted insults, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable in this project's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Respect privacy. Do not share another person's personal information, private messages, or study records without permission. Keep credentials, private question banks, confidential exam content, and identifying details out of comments, logs, and screenshots. Follow the [contribution guide](https://github.com/tigerrabbit/cloudcue/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) when proposing material.

To raise a conduct concern, [open a minimal, non-sensitive GitHub issue requesting maintainer follow-up](https://github.com/tigerrabbit/cloudcue/issues/new). Do not post private evidence or identifying details; share sensitive information only after a suitable follow-up method is arranged. Maintainers may request changes, remove comments, close threads, or restrict participation when needed. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); the security-reporting route is not a conduct contact.
