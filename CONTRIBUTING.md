# Contributing

Help improve question accuracy and explanations, keyboard and screen-reader access, or testing on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/tigerrabbit/cloudcue/issues), then [report a reproducible problem](https://github.com/tigerrabbit/cloudcue/issues/new), [propose a question](https://github.com/tigerrabbit/cloudcue/issues/new?template=question-submission.yml), or send a focused pull request. Include the problem, expected behavior, and useful non-sensitive evidence. Follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Questions and rights

Offer original contributions under [MIT](LICENSE); document redistribution authorization and retain applicable terms and notices for third-party material. Identify its author, license or permission, required attribution, and any substantial AI assistance. Question submissions need the topic, prompt, four distinct choices, correct answer, explanation of the answer and distractors, and an authoritative public source supporting the concept. Public access, purchase, a citation, or AI generation does not establish copying rights. Do not submit real or live exam questions, recalled or reconstructed items, leaked or confidential exam content, exam dumps, or copied commercial question-bank or course material.

Human maintainer review of rights, provenance, accuracy, rationale, duplicates, and scope is required before curated inclusion. Reported or identified violations are removed from the shared bank and generated copy; report concerns by question ID and reason without reposting restricted content. For accepted changes, edit `data/ccsp.psv` and relevant `data/sources.json` metadata, then run `python3 scripts/build-bank.py` and include the generated bank. Preserve applicable attribution and notices; see [content provenance](CONTENT_PROVENANCE.md). CloudCue is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISC2.

## Changes and evidence

Disclose substantial AI assistance in your issue or pull request and explain how you checked the result. You remain responsible for accuracy, rights, security, and the changes you submit. For code changes, run `npm test` and `npm run check`; native or packaging changes also need the relevant Rust and build checks in the [README](README.md#run-and-build). For documentation changes, check links and factual consistency. Give your tested OS and architecture, check results and limitations, and screenshots for UI changes. Exercise the affected study flow, including feedback, pause/resume, retries, and confirmations when relevant; use synthetic data in evidence.

Keep study offline, text rendering safe, the CSP restrictive, and private-bank storage and scores separate. Preserve confirmation before import replacement and the explicit download/copy action after export preview. Do not add automatic uploads or claim local records are encrypted. Keep lockfiles and third-party notices; exclude credentials, personal records, private banks or exports, local toolchains, dependencies, and build outputs. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); keep sensitive exploit details out of public issues. Conduct concerns use the ordinary contact described in the code of conduct.
